# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard
We are committed to a respectful, focused, and constructive community.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback technical and actionable.
- Respect privacy and security constraints.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private data without permission.
- Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations.
